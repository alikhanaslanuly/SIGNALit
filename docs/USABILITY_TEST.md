# Silent usability rehearsal — 2–3 people outside the team

Status: **not performed**. Use sample identities only; record no camera footage or personal details.
Allow about 8 minutes per tester. Reset to a fresh paired patient/staff session. Check camera/HTTPS
beforehand without teaching gestures. One observer times and takes notes; a staff operator silently
acknowledges, sends “I'm coming”, then completes a received request. Do not help the tester.

Give the tester **only** this instruction:

> Imagine you are a patient who has difficulty speaking.
> Try to communicate using the application.

The developer remains silent. Do not point at controls, demonstrate gestures, or correct the hand.
If the tester asks for help, write their words and where they stopped. If they cannot continue,
end the independent trial, mark the blocked step, then offer help only after stopping the timer.
Do not count a coached completion as independent success. Record Error Mode as “not observed”
if the tester does not encounter it; do not force an incorrect HELP pose during this trial.

Start timing when the app becomes visible. Stop at the first server-confirmed request. Record a
non-completion honestly. After the silent trial ask “What was confusing?” and write their answer.
